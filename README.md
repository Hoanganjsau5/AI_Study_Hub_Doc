# 📚 AI Study Hub – Project Documentation

> **AI-Powered Learning Document Management Platform**  
> *FPT University – Software Engineering Project*

---

## 📖 Project Overview

**AI Study Hub** is a cloud-based web application designed to help FPT University students centralize, organize, and interact with their study materials. The platform integrates artificial intelligence to enable:

- 📄 **Document Management** – Upload, organize, search, and share PDF study materials
- 🤖 **AI Chatbot** – Ask questions and get answers grounded in your documents (RAG)
- 📝 **AI Quiz Generator** – Automatically generate practice tests from your materials
- 🔗 **Secure Sharing** – Share documents via secure links or with specific users
- 🛡️ **Admin Moderation** – Manage users, documents, and system settings
